Veloci Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026 · Applies to the Veloci Android app (app.veloci), version 1.0.0 and later.

Veloci is a driving, GPS and connectivity companion (speedometer, trip computer, activity tracker, GNSS monitor and internet speed test) made by dawg-codes. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and its developer runs no server that receives your data. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What Veloci uses, and where it stays

Information Why Where it is kept
Precise location (GPS position, speed, heading, altitude, accuracy) To show your speed and, when you record, your trips, walks, runs, rides and hikes Live positions are processed in memory. Recorded routes are saved in a private database on your phone.
Trips and activities (routes, times, distance, speeds, stops, names you give them) Your history, summaries, charts and maps On your phone only
Steps and cadence from the phone's step counter (optional, with the "Physical activity" permission) Walk, run and hike statistics On your phone, with the activity
Heart rate and steps from Health Connect (optional, read-only, only when you tap "Add from Health Connect") Adding them to a finished activity On your phone, with that activity. Veloci never writes to Health Connect and reads only heart rate and steps for that activity's time.
Odometer and maintenance reminders (total distance driven, trip meters, reminders you add) The odometer and service reminders On your phone
Internet test results (speeds, latency, connection type, Wi-Fi band or mobile carrier and signal, your internet provider's name and AS number, the test server's city, labels you add) Your internet test history (Signal → Internet) On your phone only. No IP address or location is stored with them.
Driving analysis (driving scores, hard braking and acceleration, time over your speed limit, statistics) Trip insights and statistics, calculated from your recorded trips On your phone only
Vehicle for trip cost (fuel or electric, efficiency, price, currency) and each trip's estimated cost Trip cost estimates On your phone only
Parking spot (the location you save, when, its accuracy and an optional note) Finding your car On your phone only. Never in backups or bug reports. "Navigate to car" passes its coordinates (not the note) to the map app you choose.
Settings (units, theme, limits and so on) Your preferences On your phone

Veloci does not use your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, advertising ID or account information.

What leaves your phone

Veloci itself sends nothing to its developer. Information leaves your phone only in these cases:

  1. Maps. When you view a map, the map images ("tiles") for the area on screen are downloaded from the map provider, OpenFreeMap (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors) or, in builds that use it, Google Maps. Like any website, the provider receives your IP address and which map areas are requested, and can therefore infer the approximate area you are looking at, which during a live map is near where you are. Your recorded route is not sent; it is drawn on your phone. You can turn the map off in Settings → Trips & maps. These providers' own privacy policies apply to their servers: OpenFreeMap and, for Google Maps, Google's Privacy Policy.
  2. Road matching (not available in this version). Veloci contains an optional comparison feature that would send a trip's positions, only when you tap "Compare with road-matched route", to a road-matching service and on to the Google Roads API. It needs a build with Google Maps and a service that you set up yourself, so it is switched off and cannot be used in this version of the app. If it is ever offered, this policy will be updated first, and it would remain optional and used only when you tap Compare.
  3. Internet test. Only when you tap Start test (Signal → Internet), Veloci downloads and uploads test data to Cloudflare's speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com), which picks its nearest data centre. Like any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address and a user agent naming Veloci and its version. Veloci also asks Cloudflare which provider and data centre the connection goes through; its reply includes your IP address and approximate location, of which Veloci keeps only the provider's name and AS number and the data centre's city. No identifier, location, result or other personal data is sent, and no cookies are kept. A test can use a lot of data; on mobile data Veloci asks first and offers a quick test capped at 60 MB. Nothing runs in the background or on a schedule. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to its servers.
  4. Exports. If you export a trip (GPX, CSV, JSON), the file goes only where you choose to save or share it. Importing (Veloci exports or GPX files from other apps) reads only the files you pick, on your phone.
  5. Backups. If you make a backup (Settings → Backup & Restore), the .veloci file contains your trips and activities with their routes, heart rate and steps, your internet test results and your settings (including your vehicle and fuel prices for trip cost). It never contains your parking spot. It is made on your phone and goes only where you choose to save or share it; Veloci never uploads it. Because it contains your location history, keep it somewhere private.
  6. Bug reports. If you use Settings → Help & feedback → Report a bug, Veloci opens your own email app with a report addressed to the developer (dev.dawgcodes@gmail.com). Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and you can read and edit the whole email first (also in Veloci's "Preview report"). It contains what you wrote and, unless you turn it off, diagnostic information: - the app, Android and phone model and version; - Veloci's settings and state, such as whether a trip is recording or GPS is locked, and permission states; - battery, connection type and free storage and memory; - recent errors Veloci recorded in this session, and the last crash from the past week. These are recorded on your phone only, with personal details removed.

It never includes your location, coordinates, routes, trip history, GPX data, Wi-Fi network names, IP address, mobile carrier or internet provider, phone number, accounts, contacts, files or advertising and device identifiers. A screenshot is attached only if you pick one, and Veloci attaches a re-encoded copy without its location or other hidden details. Your email provider and the developer's then handle the email like any other.

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Veloci does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Backups

Veloci turns off Android's cloud backup for all of its data. When you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, only your settings are copied, not your trips or activities. To keep or move them, make a backup file yourself in Settings → Backup & Restore (see above).

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single trip, several, or all trips in the app (History). Uninstalling Veloci, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything Veloci stored. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us. Data you shared yourself (exported files) and data in Health Connect are managed by you and by Health Connect.

Security

Your data lives in Veloci's private app storage, which other apps cannot read, protected by your phone's lock screen and Android's storage encryption. Veloci does not add its own encryption on top. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Permissions

Location (to measure speed; only while Veloci is open or a trip/floating speedometer is running, with a visible notification; never "all the time"), notifications (trip status), physical activity (optional step counting), Health Connect heart rate and steps (optional, read-only), display over other apps (optional floating speedometer), and Internet and network state (to download maps, and to run the internet test and describe the connection it ran on).

Children

Veloci is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
